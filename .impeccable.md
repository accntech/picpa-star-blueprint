## Design Context

### Users
PICPA STAR Region leaders, chapter officers, committee chairs, and active members presenting a membership development blueprint to CPA communities. They need a polished deck that can guide a live discussion, align chapters around practical initiatives, and make membership feel valuable beyond dues and compliance.

### Brand Personality
Professional, civic, and energizing. The interface should feel like an official regional strategy presentation: credible enough for officers and senior CPAs, warm enough to invite young CPAs into the organization, and action-oriented enough to turn ideas into chapter commitments.

### Aesthetic Direction
A presentation-first digital deck with a ceremonial civic feel: light paper surfaces, deep green institutional ink, gold recognition accents, and supporting clay, teal, plum, and leaf tones. Use the PICPA logo from the blueprint, strong STAR typography, structured grids, and visible progress through the 90-day membership journey.

### Design Principles
- Make every slide presentation-ready at first glance, with a clear speaker focal point.
- Translate dense blueprint content into scannable groups, flows, and commitments.
- Treat STAR as both an acronym and a visual system: Strengthen, Train, Advance, Recognize.
- Keep the tone official but human, emphasizing belonging, relationships, visibility, and leadership.
- Make navigation effortless for live presenting: keyboard controls, overview mode, and quick slide selection.
